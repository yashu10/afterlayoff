About us

We turned a setback into a launchpad

After Layoff is an IT company built by engineers who believe the best response to bad news is to build something better.

Our story

Why "After Layoff"?

Layoffs hit our industry hard. Talented people lost their jobs overnight, through no fault of their own. We were among them.

Instead of waiting for the next offer, we decided to build the company we always wanted to work for — one that values craft over hype, transparency over jargon, and people over process.

The upward arrow in our logo rises out of the letter A, just like we rose from a setback. That energy goes into every project we deliver.

“A layoff isn't the end of the story. It's the plot twist.”
— The After Layoff team
