How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. A simple business website can start in the low thousands; larger products are quoted after a free discovery call. You always get a fixed or capped estimate before we start.

How long does it take?

Websites typically take 2–6 weeks, MVPs 8–12 weeks. We share a timeline in the proposal and track it openly.

You do. All source code, designs and IP are transferred to you on final payment.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes — monthly maintenance plans cover updates, monitoring, bug fixes and small improvements.

Absolutely. We're remote-first and work across time zones with overlap hours agreed up front.
