Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how After Layoff ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you visit our website or contact us about our services. By using this website, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

After Layoff is an IT services company offering web and mobile development, UI/UX design, AI & automation, cloud & DevOps, social media design and graphic design. You can reach us at hello@afterlayoff.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, project details and budget range when you use our contact form or email us; your résumé and details when you apply for a role.
  • Business information: files, briefs, credentials and content you share while we work on your project.
  • Technical information: basic data your browser sends automatically, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, which may be recorded in server logs by our hosting provider.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18, and we do not ask for sensitive data such as payment card numbers, passwords or government ID through this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and prepare proposals or quotes
  • To deliver, support and improve our services
  • To send project updates, invoices and important notices
  • To review job applications
  • To keep our website secure and prevent misuse
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Contact form

Our contact form opens your own email application with your message pre-filled. The message is sent from your email account to ours, so it is handled by your email provider and ours.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

6. Third-party services

We use a few third-party services to run this website:

  • Google Fonts — your browser loads fonts from Google's servers, which means Google can see your IP address.
  • Hosting provider — serves the website and may keep standard access logs.
  • Social platforms — if you click LinkedIn or WhatsApp share links or visit our Behance/LinkedIn profiles, those services' own privacy policies apply.

We may also use tools such as email, cloud storage and project-management software to work with you. We only share information with providers that need it to help us serve you.

7. Sharing your information

We share personal information only with (a) service providers acting on our behalf, (b) professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers, (c) authorities when required by law, or (d) a successor if our business is sold or restructured. Client project files stay confidential and are never shared with other clients.

8. Data retention

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterwards. Client records and invoices are kept for the period required by tax and accounting laws. Job applications are kept for up to 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures — such as access controls and secure connections — to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the GDPR/UK GDPR), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent or object to certain processing
  • Request a copy of your data, or raise a complaint with your data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@afterlayoff.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

We work remotely with clients worldwide, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we take steps to ensure it remains protected.
